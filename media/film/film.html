G4 gas meter · index
Every 0.01 m³ counts.
The last red drum moves one litre per digit. One full turn is one hundredth of a cubic metre.
One magnet.
One pulse.
A magnet on the last drum closes a reed contact once per turn. 0.01 m³ in, one pulse out.
Counted at the edge.
ESP32-S3 firmware debounces every pulse and saves the counter to flash on each one: two slots, CRC-32, nothing lost on a power cut.
Tehran time · UTC+03:30
It reads itself.
At 00:00 on the first day of each billing period, monthly or seasonal, the meter freezes its own reading. No visit, no estimate.
