Scan the QR code on a Smart Gas Meter invoice, or type the token printed under it.

No invoice at hand? .

What this check proves

  1. The QR code holds only an opaque token, signed with HMAC-SHA256 when the invoice is issued. It carries no name, address or amount.
  2. The token is looked up in the issuer's records. A match shows the amount and period the issuer actually billed, so a forged or edited printout does not match.
  3. This demo publishes the records of a synthetic 240-meter fleet. Prices are illustrative placeholders, not an official tariff.
